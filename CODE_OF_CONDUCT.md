# Code of Conduct

FireTheDJ is a friendly project, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else in this repository) to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their way.
- Respect people's time: search before opening an issue, and keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using the project to promote piracy or break other services' terms.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
block people who repeatedly or seriously do. To report a problem, open an issue or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code applies to everyone in project spaces, maintainer included.
