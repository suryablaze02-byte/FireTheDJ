## 4. Patterns: what makes their transitions work

Patterns 1–3 rest on adi_wav-specific evidence. Patterns 4–6 are **general** research on how DJs in this edit- and mashup-heavy, cross-genre style work, and are labelled as such.

1. **The edit is the transition (adi_wav, high confidence).** The genre jump is pre-built: a familiar South Asian vocal over a club genre's drums at that genre's tempo. In the set it then mixes like any UKG, afro or DnB track. The same desi-edit movement is behind the Delhi duo MadStarBase, whose Bollywood bootlegs started as edits "for their own sets" and later got Universal and Saregama deals. [SoundCloud; IG; Ticket Fairy]
2. **Vocal from one deck over the other deck's beat (adi_wav, medium).** "Mashup" on every music reel, a merged audio title ("Why this hola amigo?"), and a viewer noticing "a vocal loaded on the other deck". Digital DJ Tips calls this the **acapella bridge**: start a well-known vocal as one track ends, swap the beat underneath partway through, and the vocal carries the floor across a genre, mood or energy change. Rules: same or compatible key, within about 5 BPM, start the vocal on an obvious structural point (breakdown or drop). [IG; Digital DJ Tips acapellas]
3. **Recognition over genre fit (adi_wav, medium, partly inference).** The pairings are hook against hook from very different worlds: Tamil 2011 viral × Hindi DHH 2023, UK grime-house × Delhi rap, Punjabi pop × DnB. The "oil & water" series title says it outright: the clash is the point, and familiarity on both sides is what makes it land. Viewers reply with pairing requests ("try Boom Shaka and Kolaveri Di", "Victory Lap x Swah"), which shows the audience judges these mixes by *which two songs* meet.
4. **Half-time / double-time links (general).** Hip-hop at 70–90 lines up with 140–180 genres in half time. Digital DJ Tips cites 156 ↔ 78 for dubstep ↔ hip-hop. This is what makes DHH ↔ UK bass / DnB mashups possible without audible stretching.
5. **Switch genres on the biggest hook, or through silence (general).** Digital DJ Tips: drop beatmixing and change genre and BPM on a track's biggest hook or drop; or cut the music, bring in an acapella at the new BPM, then the beat. Crossfader: breakdowns that fall to near silence are the natural place to change genre and BPM; for wide gaps, loop four beats and ride the tempo down, then start the new track on a phrase. [DDJT tempo tips; Crossfader]
6. **Stems: drums before melody (general).** Stem guides describe moving between genres by swapping the drums first and the melody second, plus stem-only effects (vocal echo while the beat cuts). Pros pre-separate their key tracks offline because real-time separation has artefacts. [dj.studio; Serato Stems review]

## 5. How FireTheDJ can reach that level

Checked against the current code (working tree, 2026-10-28). Ranked by value. No vocal-tag or voice-drop features are proposed.

| Rank | Recommendation | Status | Files | What would change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cross-genre vocal bridge ("mashup swap")**: B's sung hook rides over A's beat, then the beats swap. This is the core adi_wav move. | **Partial.** `VOCAL_SWAP` exists, but `DecisionEngine.scores` sets it to −∞ unless `beatmatched && keysFit`, and the cross-genre rule then takes −1.0 off every non-cut recipe, so it almost never fires across genres. Its entry is B's *first* sung phrase (`firstVocal`), not B's hook. | `dj/MixRecipes.kt`, `dj/TransitionPlanner.kt` | Exempt `VOCAL_SWAP` from the cross-genre penalty (give it a bonus instead when the families differ and the tempos relate at 1×, ½× or 2×; `tempoMultiple` already folds those). Add `b.hookBar` as an entry candidate when its vocalness clears `VOCAL`. Consider a sibling `HOOK_BRIDGE` recipe: A's own hook vocal is held over B's beat for 4–8 bars, then A drops out (the reverse direction). |
| 2 | **On-device stem separation** (vocals, drums, instrumental), offline at analysis time. | **Missing** (listed as a roadmap item in `HOW_IT_WORKS.md`). `VOCAL_CUT` and `VOCAL_ISOLATE` are mid/side + EQ approximations, so centred drums and bass bleed. A vocal bridge over a *different genre's* beat exposes that bleed far more than a same-genre swap. | `analysis/` (new separator), `dj/fx/FxChain.kt`, `dj/fx/FxPlan.kt` | Separate offline once per track (cache with the analysis), so phone CPU limits don't matter at playback. Every item from rank 1 up to rank 6 sounds better with it; rank 3 needs it outright. |
| 3 | **Stem-wise swap: drums first, melody and vocal second**, for genre changes. | **Missing.** `FxPlan` has only a gain lane and a bass lane per deck; the beat can't be crossfaded separately from the music. | `dj/fx/FxPlan.kt` (add drum and vocal lanes), `dj/MixRecipes.kt` (new recipe) | A cross-genre recipe: B's drums replace A's on a phrase start while A's vocal and melody carry on for 4 bars, then the rest swaps. That is how a "but UKG" edit sounds, done live. |
| 4 | **Let far-apart genres meet when a hook link or half-time link justifies it** ("oil & water"). | **Partial.** `SetPlanner.transitionCost` adds 14 for genre distance 2 (e.g. Bollywood/POP ↔ DnB), and the word-link bonus only applies at distance ≤ 1 (`wordFriendly`). So DHH ↔ DnB or Tamil film ↔ UK bass pairings are effectively never picked, even in "All genres" mode. | `dj/SetPlanner.kt`, `analysis/Genres.kt` | In All-genres mode, cut the distance-2 cost to about 6 when the tempos link at ½× / 1× / 2× within stretch, the keys fit, and B has a strong sung hook (`vocalIn(hookBar)`). Rate-limit it (e.g. at most one per 4 tracks) so it stays a highlight. Optionally add `DNB` and `BASS` as neighbours of `POP` when either track is Indian, since desi-to-bass is a recognised scene. |
| 5 | **Read edits by their target genre, not their language.** "Sahiba — Afro House (ADI edit)", "Khuda Jaane but DnB", "Tera Nasha but UKG" | **Partial / risk.** `Genres.region()` marks a track Indian from title words and names ("bollywood", "punjabi", "desi", "Anirudh"…). `SfxDressing.indian` then forces `DRUM_FILL_CUT`, `indianCuts` and dappu/dhol phrase fills, even on a DnB or afro-house edit. Edit-heavy desi libraries like ADI's would get roadshow drums on UKG. | `analysis/Genres.kt`, `dj/SfxDressing.kt`, `dj/MixRecipes.kt` | Detect edit markers in titles (`but <genre>`, `<genre> edit/flip/remix`, `(… edit)`) and let the named genre set the family. When the audio family is DnB, house/UKG, bass or Latin-afro, keep Indian dressing out of the transition recipes, or at most allow one regional sting per track. |
| 6 | **Hook-to-hook cut across genres** (switch on A's biggest hook straight into B's hook or drop, no beatmatching). | **Partial.** `HARD_CUT`, `DROP_SWAP` and `WORD_PLAY` exist. Entry candidates include `firstPhrase`, `introEndBar`, `firstVocal`, `bassDropEntry` and `tempoEntries`, but not B's `hookBar`, and B may only be entered in its first 40%. | `dj/TransitionPlanner.kt` | Add a `hookBar` entry for cuts. For cross-genre cuts, prefer the exit right after A's hook phrase ends (`a.hookBar + 8`) when it falls inside the play window. |
| 7 | **Acapella-through-silence bridge for big tempo gaps** (A's beat stops; A's vocal phrase finishes dry or echoed; B enters at its own tempo). | **Partial.** `WordPlay`'s `ECHO` and `PAUSE` bridges do a version of this, but only when a shared word exists. `LOOP_RAMP` covers gaps up to ±45% by ramping. | `dj/MixRecipes.kt`, `dj/TransitionPlanner.kt` | A non-lyric variant: at a phrase end where A is sung, cut A's drums (needs rank 2), let the vocal line plus echo finish, and drop B on its downbeat. It bridges any tempo gap without a ramp. |
| 8 | **Synthesized "flip" bridge beat** (emulate a "but UKG/DnB" edit for a few bars): a synthesized 2-step / DnB break / afro-house percussion loop at B's tempo under A's isolated vocal, then B. | **Missing.** `SfxSynth` already synthesizes dappu, marfa, dhol, thavil, snare rolls and impacts, but no Western groove loops. | `dj/fx/SfxSynth.kt`, `dj/fx/FxPlan.kt` (new `FxType`s), `dj/MixRecipes.kt` | The most "adi_wav" idea, but the riskiest: a synthesized beat under a real vocal can sound cheap. Prototype only after rank 2. **Speculative.** |
| 9 | **Half-time / double-time locking for overlaps.** | **Have.** `TransitionPlanner.tempoMultiple` picks 0.5, 1 or 2; `VOCAL_SWAP` and blends work at those multiples. | — | No change needed. Rank 1 should make use of it. |
| 10 | **Key fit before any vocal overlap.** | **Have.** `keyShift` pitches B by up to ±2 st, `VOCAL_SWAP` requires `keysFit`, and `keyLift` rewards stepping up the wheel. | — | Keep the requirement for rank 1. The general sources stress key matching for acapella layering. |

Not recommended: more SFX or vocal tags. None of ADI's documented transitions rely on one-shots, and vocal tags are excluded by the user.
